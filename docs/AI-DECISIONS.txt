# Architectural Decisions

- Target framework remains .NET Framework 4.7.2 to match RimWorld's modding
  sandbox.
- Solution intentionally links metadata XML and documentation assets for
  visibility in Visual Studio.
- Added an xUnit-based regression project that validates XML defs without
  needing RimWorld binaries so Codex can run automated checks in CI-friendly
  environments.

2026-10-12 Claude: Batshittium Ballista (v0.7), spec agreed with Matt
"Breakpoint" Heck: power is linear in units = count x range / 10, from
50 W standby / 1,000 W per volley at the baseline to 62.5 kW / 990 kW at
256 bolts and 325 tiles (a quarter of one reactor at standby; four reactors
less 10 kW of magazine feeding at full fire). Implementation choices:
- Subclass Building_TurretGun; give each Ballista its own clone of the
  gun's VerbProperties so range is per building (vanilla shares one per
  Def). Range presets skip 80-89 because vanilla draws cell rings below 90
  tiles and the engine caps them at ~79.8; at 90+ a circle outline is drawn.
- The firing draw is a one-second PowerOutput spike per volley. Before a
  volley the grid must cover it from surplus or stored battery energy, or
  the Ballista holds fire with a message.
- Bolt stores (16 internal, 2,560 per magazine) are bookcase-style haul
  destinations. ThingOwner accepts any amount, so CappedThingOwner caps
  GetCountCanAccept and TryAdd; haul jobs size themselves from it.
- Magazines connect by shared edges, chained (Core.MagazineNetwork); a
  volley drains magazines nearest-first, then the internal reserve.
- The Artificer is a comp on a plain building fed by CompRefuelable;
  outputs are gated per bolt research.

2026-10-13 Claude: Self-destruct aftermath, after Matt "Breakpoint" Heck
found the blast "just sort of a shockwave". Root cause: in 1.6 a Bomb
explosion destroys every Fire it touches (DamageWorker.ExplosionDamageThing),
and the old code lit the fire blanket at detonation, so the expanding wave
put it out. MapComponent_SelfDestructAftermath now lights the same fires
(same chance and size) three ticks behind the wave front, using vanilla's
timing (1.5 ticks per tile at speed 1), and sets off secondary explosions
at 15 per second inside the expanding circle and for 4 s after. The
secondaries are XML entries (Flame bursts, BombSuper at 40 and 80 damage);
BombSuper looks like Bomb but is not caught by the fire check. Timing and
sampling live in Core.BlastSchedule with unit tests. The aftermath is saved
with the map.
2026-10-13 Claude: Self-destruct generalized and destruction made
interesting, per Matt "Breakpoint" Heck. CompSelfDestruct (the last comp,
because comps keep ticking after a mid-loop Kill) owns the order, arming
job, countdown, alarm, flee, rings and a new warning band: a one-cell
surround fading sinusoidally in yellow-orange (1 s period), red and 0.25 s
in the final 3 s; alpha is quantized to 12 levels because RimWorld pools a
material per colour (Core.CountdownPulse, unit tested). What happens at
zero is a BatshitDetonation subclass chosen in XML with Class=. The
replicator keeps its bomb, fires and secondaries; the reactor launches
3 x 256 Really Painful bolts at once (a Ballista volley's look; flight time
grows with distance, so impacts spread as a wave). Every damaging hit has a
5% chance to trigger a self-destruct, half of those skipping the countdown
(detonating on the next tick, after the hit finishes applying); an armed
device destroyed by damage goes off, as vanilla explosives do. Devices
without a self-destruct get a CompDeathBlast with their own blast on
KillFinalize: hopper burst, psychic squeeze, bolt cook-off (bolts kept
rather than spilled, up to a cap; the rest pulverized), molten spray.
Non-weapon devices got their own 500-point projects after Lore (Lore now
unlocks nothing directly). The Ballista's player-facing name is Batshit
Ballista; its defName stays BT_BatshittiumBallista so the playtest save
keeps working. The Def validator now honours Class= on plain fields, as
the game's loader does.
