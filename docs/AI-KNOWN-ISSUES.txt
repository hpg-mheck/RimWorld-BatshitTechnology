# Known Issues

- Reactor waste heat (1,000 per second over 12 tiles, capped at 200 C) is
  estimated from vanilla heater numbers and needs tuning in play.
- The Random Replicator borrows the vanilla Infinite Chemreactor sprite;
  the Batshittium textures are procedural placeholders.
- A replicator storing food at a priority above every stockpile keeps it
  inside, where nobody eats it. The default priority is Low and the
  auto-eject toggle covers colonies without a food stockpile.
