# Known Issues

- Reactor waste heat (1,000 per second over 12 tiles, capped at 200 C) is
  estimated from vanilla heater numbers and needs tuning in play.
- All textures are procedural placeholders (Tools/make_textures.py,
  following docs/ARTISTIC-MOTIF.md); hand-drawn art may replace them.
- A replicator storing food at a priority above every stockpile keeps it
  inside, where nobody eats it. The default priority is Low and the
  auto-eject toggle covers colonies without a food stockpile.
- The self-destruct aftermath (about 90 secondary explosions and a few
  thousand fires over six seconds on the smoke-test map) costs about 9 ms
  per tick while it runs; fine at normal speed, sluggish at speed 3. The
  fire afterwards costs about 1 ms per tick.
- The reactor's barrage (768 explosive bolts) costs about 5 ms per tick
  for the six seconds it takes to land.
- Fire counts as damage, so a burning self-destruct device rolls its 5%
  trigger on every fire tick and will almost certainly go off. That is
  probably the most batshit outcome, but worth watching in play.
- Multi-bomb physics (2026-10-14): pulls of constant strength cancel all
  along the line between two equal centres, so things in an overlap
  gather on that line and creep along it at a few per cent of the rate;
  both bombs then run to their 8 s maximum. A bomb planted within
  another's reach makes its own pile a balance point too: a thing at its
  centre leaves it only if the other pull is the one it favours. Both
  are the physics as specified, and worth watching in play.
- Stretching acts on any pawn under more than one strong pull, colonists
  included; three points a second between two bombs head on will hurt
  but rarely kill in eight seconds. Tune in play.
- The singularity moves pawns by teleporting them a cell at a time (with
  the draw position tweened), and holds every pawn in reach stunned from
  the moment it opens; there is no true physics, and natural rock is never
  ripped loose. Items pile one stack per cell (or merge), so a big pile
  spreads into a blob rather than a single heap.
- The smoke test fires the Ballista at a forced ground target only; its
  automatic targeting of real raiders is vanilla code but untested here.
- The Ballista holds fire under a roof (its bolts arc overhead), as vanilla
  mortars do.
- Smoke flakes seen on 2026-10-13 while building the implosion, each on
  one random quick-test map, and what became of them while building the
  Tractor Bomb:
  - "Not enough squares to get to radius 329" was a real bug, now fixed:
    raiders path round ai_combatDangerous turrets on an avoid grid out
    to the turret's range plus 4, past the engine's radial limit of about
    79.8 for a Ballista set beyond 75 tiles. It showed whenever raiders
    pathed while a Ballista was set long (the smoke test sets 325 for
    700 ticks), so in play it would have logged during every raid. The
    Ballista is no longer combatDangerous; a smoke check regenerates the
    avoid grid with a 325-tile Ballista, and failed with this error
    before the fix.
  - The Artificer's "+0 bolts" was map noise in the test: haulers carry
    the bolts off as they appear (one carrying a merged stack of twenty
    at the check reads as +0), and the Ballista spends painful bolts on
    any hostile in range. The count now includes carried and pocketed
    bolts and bolts fired.
  - "then settles" was map noise: pawns walking into the reach of a pull
    on a burning map. The runner now puts out every fire and drafts the
    colonists first.
  - The runner also silences the storyteller for the whole run, so no
    raid or other incident lands mid-check, and notes any hostiles
    present at the start (none so far).
- Smoke flakes still unexplained (the checks now report more when they
  fail):
  - "AnyRim's devices and lamps are powered": one to five standing lamps
    on a power net with 0 W surplus, cut off from the town reactor. Seen
    once before the Tractor Barn work, then three times in about thirty
    runs during it, at sites where it passed on other runs, so it is not
    the site alone. The check now names the nets: the reactor was
    producing 250 kW and the town's consumers sat on two nets, one of
    them with no generator. It also lists conduits lost since the build
    and fires in the town, to tell a broken plan from a conduit
    destroyed after it (not yet seen with those). AnyRim runs before any
    Tractor Bomb code.
  - "the colonist sat in the squeezer": the colonist's job ended before
    it reached the chair, twelve tiles off, before any Tractor Bomb code
    ran. Once more (2026-10-13) it sat but left before the 600 ticks its
    effects need; the check now feeds and rests the colonist first and
    reports how long it sat.
  - Seen again while building the multi-bomb physics (about twenty
    runs on 2026-10-14): the AnyRim lamp check three times, and once the
    Artificer's "it works the next unit on its own" (bolts +4, 28 fired,
    none carried), both before any implosion code runs.
  - The Random Replicator was gone, with nothing logged, when its
    detonation test began, after AnyRim, and the runner crashed (once in
    about thirty runs). The runner now fails a check saying how it went
    (destroyed, held, hit points, fires near it) and carries on with a
    fresh replicator.
- "AnyRim builds at a scored site" failed once (2026-10-13) on a map
  with an insect hive and eight hostile insects: the site rules keep the
  town away from hostiles, and with the map's edges, geysers and a void
  monolith no site anywhere qualified. That is the site search working
  as designed on an unlucky map, not a fault; rerun.
- The Tractor Bomb (docs/TRACTOR-BOMB.md, "As Built"):
  - A Timid robot stops 15 tiles from its target, so with an Intimate
    (6-tile) pull a target that stands still escapes, and at Neighborly
    (15) it is only just inside; Prudent, the default, stops inside the
    pull.
  - A planted bomb cannot be stopped: a hit only starts its pull, or sets
    off its blast, at once.
  - The protest beep is vanilla's TrapArm click until the sound pipeline
    exists.
  - Robots appear in no vanilla list (colonist bar, animals or mechs
    tab): find them on the map.
  - The Tractor Barn takes no new bill while its repair hopper is empty
    (a vanilla work table with a refuelable comp refuses bills then), so
    the hopper always refills and cannot be switched off. A build
    already under way finishes.
  - One robot docks at a barn at a time; another below half health keeps
    patrolling and tries again after each patrol leg.
  - A pawn that has run past a bomb's reach may wander back into it
    before the pull (a muffalo did, in the smoke test); it runs again on
    its next think if it can see the bomb.
  - A hostile that rolls "ignore" keeps attacking even inside the blast
    radius, and the pull takes it if it is in reach: by design.
  - Its sprites are top-down like the mod's buildings, not the
    three-quarter view of vanilla pawns, so a tractor turning east shows
    its side from above.
- Showcases: with -quicktest -showcase=<def>, the showcase runs on
  vanilla's 250-cell quick-test map, ignoring the def's mapSize and biome
  (a MAPSIZE or BIOME cue says so). Applying them would need a second
  Harmony patch; the main menu's Start and plain -showcase= apply both.
- Showcases: Stop ends narration at once, so a "stopped" narration line
  with a delay is never said (it is a configuration error, so a def
  author sees it in dev mode).
- Showcases: the caption bar, the windows and Force start's confirmation
  have been checked by the smoke runner's direct calls, not yet with a
  mouse at 2x UI scale on a small screen.
- Showcases: a showcase's DEV buttons disappear from the throwaway colony
  when it stops or finishes, because god mode is given back then; turn
  god mode on from the dev toolbar to keep using them.
- Showcases (2026-10-14): the narration overlay is not clickable, so a
  line no longer jumps the camera to what it is about, as a vanilla
  message did; the director frames it already. Lines said less than
  2.5 s apart queue and show in turn, so a burst of lines lags the
  action by up to 2.5 s a line (the tour verifier's pacing NOTE gives
  the longest wait at normal speed). The overlay has been checked by the
  smoke runner in a small window, not yet by eye at 2x UI scale on 4K.
- Tour, the stampede (2026-10-14, slice 14000): the scene declines on a
  map whose east edge has too little open ground that reaches the base,
  even after widening the band and sliding it north (it never slides
  south, toward the tractor yard); one sabotage run declined so, before
  the slides were added. The Ballista fires
  only once the herd is within 76 tiles, the most the demo base's one
  reactor powers for 256 bolts, so the herd walks about 13 s at normal
  speed first. The tick the volley lands costs 120 to 310 ms (one
  visible hitch); a herd packed tight round a target cost 785 ms in one
  sabotage run. The cleanup volleys and the "stragglers" line have
  played in one clean run (a 128-bolt volley at 51 stragglers) and in
  the sabotage runs; elsewhere the first volley killed all 200. One of
  fourteen verify runs of the stampede alone (g3-only3, map with AnyRim
  at (59,170)) fired nothing: the herd never came within reach in
  2,160 ticks and never reached the keep-away, so the scene gave up and
  sent it home, and the verifier failed the 256-bolt and 90 per cent
  facts. Not reproduced in the twelve stampede-only runs and three
  tour runs since; the cause (slow ground, a herd that did not advance, a
  grid that did not power the Ballista, or another session using the
  same data folder at the time) is not known. Its log shows one game,
  without errors. The scene now logs, every 600 ticks while the
  volley waits, the herd's centre, distance, pace, mental states and
  jobs, and the grid's numbers, so the next one will say which.

- Showcase seeds (slice 18000, 2026-10-14): a seed pins a showcase's
  world, starting tile, colonists and settlement in any process, but not
  its map: RimWorld 1.6's Map.NextGenSeed mixes System.HashCode's random
  per-process seed into the Rand blocks of ancient shrines, ruins, junk
  clusters, mechanoid remains, prefabs, layouts and unique weapons, and
  the plants, rock chunks and animals placed after them shift with them.
  So MAPHASH and STAGEHASH differ between launches of one seed, and so
  does the stage built on the map (in one pair AnyRim found a site in one
  run and none in the other). Terrain, rock, ores, geysers and roads
  repeat. The proven fix is a second Harmony patch (a deterministic
  NextGenSeed while a showcase's map generates), awaiting approval
  (docs/AI-requests-for-humans.txt, item 22). Within one process the
  same seed would repeat the map; trials run a process each.
- Even with the map and stage pinned, a tour after READY repeats only
  partly: the game draws on its one global random state from code that
  runs per frame (effects, sounds, the interface) as well as per tick,
  and how many ticks fall in each frame depends on the machine's load.
  docs/AI-TESTING-STRATEGY.txt ("Tour trials") records how far the
  verify runs repeated.
- The verifier no longer silences the storyteller at READY: the Showcase
  API holds it quiet for every showcase until FINISHED, then gives it
  back, so a raid in the farewell is possible; a colonist lost after
  FINISHED is noted, not failed.
